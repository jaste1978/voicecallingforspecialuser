## Tier 3 — everyone else on the list (about 120 accounts)

Tejas's read (28 Oct 2026): around 90% of the accounts @sunosathiapp follows
are deaf or hard of hearing people, whatever their content is about. So the
tiers above are only about reach. Everyone else gets the same short ask,
which needs no follower count:

**E. Referral ask, one message, no link (Instagram DM)**
Namaste! I am Tejas from SunoSathi, the free captioned-phone app for deaf
people in India. We are building the first open Indian Sign Language
dataset. Do you sign in ISL yourself, or know someone who does: a friend,
family, an interpreter, a teacher? Ten minutes on a phone is all it takes.
If yes, I will send you a short page that explains it.

Reply "yes" or "I know someone" → send D2 (the link). The page has a
"Send on WhatsApp" button, so a referral travels on WhatsApp from there
without them having to write anything.

**E (Hindi)**
नमस्ते! मैं तेजस हूँ, सुनोसाथी से, भारत में बहरे लोगों के लिए मुफ़्त कैप्शन
