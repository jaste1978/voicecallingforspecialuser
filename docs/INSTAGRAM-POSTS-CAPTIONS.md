# Instagram posts — photo set (13 Sep 2026)

Files: `brand/social/instagram/photo-posts/photo-post1..4.jpg` (1080×1350).
Photo-real people generated with Higgsfield Soul 2.0 (0.12 credits each) with a
blank phone screen facing the camera; the real SunoSathi screens (captions,
picture board) were composited onto the phone in perspective with
`brand/social/photos/composite-screen.py`. Raw photos and composites are in
`brand/social/photos/`. The earlier illustrated set was rejected (too cartoon,
story over product) and is kept only in git history.

ALL FOUR POSTED on @sunosathiapp on 13 Sep 2026 (order 1, 3, 2, 4), via the Claude browser bridge. Image posts upload fine from the bridge; video does not (see docs/INSTAGRAM-REELS-CAPTIONS.md).

## Post 1 — Phone calls you can read (man on verandah)
Phone calls you can read.
