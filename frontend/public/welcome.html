A normal phone call. That's the whole point.

Assistive tech usually asks the world to change. SunoSathi doesn't — the caller dials a normal number and talks normally.

1

Someone calls your number

A real Indian phone number rings your SunoSathi app — with sound, vibration and a flashing screen.

2

You read, like a chat

The caller's words appear as live chat bubbles in the language they speak — Hindi, Gujarati, English, or mixed. If a language can't be identified, it's written the way it sounds — "kem cho".

3

You reply with your voice

Speak — the caller hears you directly. Can't speak right now? One tap politely says "please repeat" or "one moment" in a natural voice.

Built for India, not translated for it

🌐

Speaks your India

Auto-detects Hindi, Gujarati, English and Hinglish mid-sentence — powered by Sarvam AI, built for Indian speech.

⚡

Truly live

Captions land in under a second at natural pauses. A real conversation, not a transcript afterwards.

📞

Calls out too

Call your contacts or any number. They see a normal call from your number — and hear your real voice.

📚

Never lose a word

Every call saved as transcript + audio. The doctor's instructions, the delivery address — always there to re-read.

🔔

Rings your way

Vibration, flashing screen and an audible ring for family nearby — every call gets noticed.

🛡️

Quality, measured

Every call is automatically re-checked for caption accuracy. We publish our numbers to ourselves before anyone else.
