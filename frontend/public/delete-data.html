Delete your data

You can delete your SunoSathi account and everything in it, anytime. आप अपना खाता और सारा डेटा कभी भी delete कर सकते हैं।

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SunoSathi and sign in.
  2. Go to Support (the help / ❓ section).
  3. Tap “Delete my account & data” and confirm with your password.

Your data is then scheduled for deletion and permanently removed within 12 hours. You can cancel from the same screen anytime before then.

Just want to clear your call history but keep your account? The same screen has a “Clear my call history” option that removes your calls, transcripts and recordings immediately.

Option 2 — by email

No longer have the app? Email us from the address on your account and we’ll delete it for you:

Email namaste@sunosathi.com

You can also WhatsApp +91 98190 95969. We verify the request comes from the account owner, then delete within 12 hours.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, phone number).
  • Your call history — transcripts, captions and audio recordings.
  • Your saved contacts and any linked numbers.
  • Notification tokens and any support/feedback messages you sent.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep only a minimal, non-personal record that a deletion took place, as required for legal compliance. This process meets Google Play and Apple account-deletion requirements and the right to erasure under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
